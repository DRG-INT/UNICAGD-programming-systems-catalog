## System Engineer Summary

📷 QuickRawPicker is a free and open source program that lets you cull, pick or rate raw photos
captured by your camera. It is also compatible with the XMP sidecar file used by Adobe
Bridge/Lightroom/Darktable or PP3 sidecar file used by Rawtherapee.
