## System Engineer Summary

Subwasm is a cli utility to look inside a Substrate WASM Runtime. It can inspect and compare the
metadata of Substrate based runtimes such as Polkadot or Kusama.

## Operational Role

For a systems engineer, chevdor/subwasm belongs in the WebAssembly inventory as part of runtime
behavior, deployment packaging, embedding, upgrade cadence, and compatibility validation.
