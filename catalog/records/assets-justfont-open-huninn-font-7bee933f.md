## System Engineer Summary

“jf open-huninn” (jf open 粉圓) is an Open-Source Traditional Chinese Rounded Typeface specially
designed for better use in Taiwan. The font includes commonly used characters in Taiwan, Bopomofo,
and even Taigi and Hokkien phonetic symbols and characters to meet local requirements.
