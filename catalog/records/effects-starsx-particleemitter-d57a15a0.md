## System Engineer Summary

An improved implementation of my CASA 2013 paper "Real‐time generation of smoothed‐particle
hydrodynamics‐based special effects in character animation" . Particle emissions from the surface of
mesh. The emitters are approximatively uniformly distributed by taking the advantage of quad
tessellations. The fluid is simulated by SPH using my prefix-sum bin sorting.
