## System Engineer Summary

A C-simulation using basilisk of a dense ring falling through air, hitting water, and sinking. As it
plunges downward, it pushes water inward beneath it, launching a vertical jet upward from the center
of the surface. The ring eventually settles on the tank floor while the jet rises, breaks up, and
the water gradually returns to rest.
