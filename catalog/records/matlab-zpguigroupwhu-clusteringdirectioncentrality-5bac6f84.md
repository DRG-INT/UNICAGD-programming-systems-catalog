## System Engineer Summary

A novel Clustering algorithm by measuring Direction Centrality (CDC) locally. It adopts a density-
independent metric based on the distribution of K-nearest neighbors (KNNs) to distinguish between
internal and boundary points. The boundary points generate enclosed cages to bind the connections of
internal points.
