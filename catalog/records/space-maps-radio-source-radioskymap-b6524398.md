## System Engineer Summary

RadioSkyMap is an desktop application designed for radio astronomy enthusiasts and amateur
astronomers. It provides a real-time sky map with (azimuth/elevation) overlaid with Neutral Hydrogen
(HI) emission data, 408 MHz synchrotron continuum maps, and major celestial radio source catalogs
(such as 3CR, Parkes, and G4Jy).
