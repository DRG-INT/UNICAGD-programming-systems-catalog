## System Engineer Summary

An image and texture viewer for tga, png, apng, exr, dds, pvr, ktx, ktx2, astc, pkm, qoi, gif, hdr,
jpg, tif, ico, webp, and bmp files. Uses Dear ImGui, OpenGL, and Tacent. Useful for game devs as it
displays information like the presence of an alpha channel and querying specific pixels for their
colour.

## Operational Role

For a systems engineer, bluescan/tacentview belongs in the Photography inventory as part of defect
discovery, security review, undefined-state detection, and regression prevention.
