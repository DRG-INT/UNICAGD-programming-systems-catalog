## System Engineer Summary

Open Sauce Fonts is a font super family that I developed for Creative Sauce's internal type system.
It is a compact typeface that is optimised for better viewing small text on screen and print. Open
Sauce fonts (Sans, One and Two) are under the SIL Open Font License and is going to be actively
developed, improved and tested.
